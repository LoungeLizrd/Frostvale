Frostvale — Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Frostvale is a single-player game. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No accounts. You do not sign in or create an account.
  • No tracking or analytics. The game contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code.
  • No network use. The game runs entirely on your device and does not send data anywhere.
  • Saved games. Your progress is saved only on your device. If you have device backups turned on (iCloud Backup or Google backup), your operating system may include the save in those backups. We cannot access them.
  • Children. The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
